# Optional bounded frontend repair service

## Offer

Launch Patch: **USD149**, up to five public pages reviewed, three prioritized findings with reproduction notes, and up to two agreed frontend fixes in a pull request. One in-scope revision is included. Delivery: five business days after the required materials and authorized repository access are available and the order has been explicitly accepted.

AI agents prepare changes, a separate AI agent reviews them, and focused automated checks validate relevant behavior. No manual human review is promised. Do not submit code if your project prohibits AI-assisted processing.

Outside scope: authentication, checkout, databases, backend rework, a full redesign, accessibility certification, rankings or revenue guarantees. Deployment requires separate explicit permission. The examples here are synthetic and are not customer references.

## Current availability

Scope inquiries are open. **Online checkout and automatic order acceptance are not enabled.** A suitable existing payment method must be confirmed before an order is accepted. No payment processor or identity-verification requirement is being bypassed.

## Public scope inquiry

Open a [scope inquiry](https://github.com/byunginhb/mobile-ui-repair-notes/issues/new?template=scope-inquiry.md). Share only a public page or minimal synthetic reproduction and the behavior you want repaired. Do not post private source, credentials, financial information, personal identification or customer data. Public issues are not a confidential support channel.

A response explains fit, boundaries and next steps. It is not a binding order or a payment request. Confidential materials and payment details require a separately agreed private channel.
